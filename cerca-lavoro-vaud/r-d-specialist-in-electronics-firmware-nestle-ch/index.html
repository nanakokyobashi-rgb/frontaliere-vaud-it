As a R&D Specialist in Electronics Firmware at Nestlé Product Technology Center for Coffee & Beverages , your mission is to own the firmware architecture and codebase for Nespresso portioned coffee machines, delivering robust, secure, and scalable embedded software across platforms. You’ll make a difference by collaborating closely with electronics, user experience, digital, procurement, operations, quality and external development partners. This is your opportunity to take ownership, grow your expertise, and make a difference at scale.

How you’ll make an impact as a R&D Specialist in Electronics Firmware

  • Leading end-to-end embedded firmware delivery from concept to production, ensuring quality, cost and timing commitments are met.
  • Driving scalable firmware architecture, secure coding practices and reusable software foundations across coffee machine platforms.
  • Building standardized development practices, including code repositories, review gates, documentation frameworks and automated workflows.
  • Delivering reliable connectivity and device-to-device communication by aligning protocols with mobile, cloud and external technology partners.
  • Strengthening project readiness through clear requirements, risk anticipation, planning discipline and strong cross-functional alignment.
  • Contributing to future technology roadmaps and building engineering capabilities that support long-term innovation.

What will make you successful

  • Bringing strong embedded firmware expertise in C, C++, real-time operating systems, drivers and microcontroller-based development.
  • Applying architecture ownership, platform thinking and code governance to create modular, secure and maintainable software.
  • Using modern firmware development practices, including continuous integration, test automation, secure updates and connected device technologies.
  • Collaborating effectively with business partners, project teams, suppliers and development partners to translate needs into clear, testable requirements.
  • Communicating fluently in English while conveying complex technical messages in a simple and engaging way for diverse audiences
  • French and/or German is a plus.
