Panoramica

Mission A postdoctoral position is available in the laboratory of Prof.

Alex Persat (EPFL School of Life Sciences, https://www.p-lab.science/ ) to establish a new image-based drug screening platform targeting gram-negative pathogens ( Klebsiella pneumoniae , Acinetobacter baumannii , Pseudomonas aeruginosa ).

The project will combine single-cell/single-bacterium imaging approaches with the lab's tissue-engineered infection models to identify and characterize compounds active against these priority pathogens.

Responsabilità principali

  • Main duties and responsibilities
  • Design and build a microscopy-based phenotypic screening pipeline (live imaging, high-content, single-cell resolution) to assay compound activity
  • Develop imaging assays capable of distinguishing mechanism of action (e.g. growth inhibition, biofilm/attachment disruption, membrane integrity)
  • Develop or adapt image analysis and quantification pipelines with AI support to handle screen-scale data
  • Follow up on screening hits to identify their molecular target or mechanism of action (e.g. via genetic, chemical-biology, or biophysical approaches), with support from other lab members' expertise in microbiology and drug discovery
  • Collaborate with the lab's discovery and design efforts (natural products, AI-enabled molecular design) to feed prioritized targets and hits back into upstream design
