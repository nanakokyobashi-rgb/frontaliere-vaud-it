The NLP & AIMM Labs at EPFL are looking for a Teaching Program Coordinator to manage the operational backbone of their teaching portfolios.

You will be the central point of coordination across three courses: CS-552 Modern Natural Language Processing CS-461 Foundation Models and Generative AI

and an upcoming bachelor's-level AI-101 (launching in Fall 2027)

  • The NLP & AIMM Labs at EPFL are looking for a Teaching Program Coordinator to manage the operational backbone of their teaching portfolios.
  • You will be the central point of coordination across three courses: CS-552 Modern Natural Language Processing CS-461 Foundation Models and Generative AI
  • Main duties and responsibilities Course operations (CS-552, CS-461, AI-101)
  • Organize and manage the TA team for each course: recruitment support, onboarding, task allocation, and weekly coordination
  • University degree (Bachelor's or Master's) in Computer Science, Data Science, or a related technical field
  • Prior experience as a TA, head TA, or in course/program coordination, ideally in a technical or CS-related field

Responsabilità principali

  • Main duties and responsibilities Course operations (CS-552, CS-461, AI-101)
  • Organize and manage the TA team for each course: recruitment support, onboarding, task allocation, and weekly coordination
  • Own the semester production calendar for assignments, midterms, and project milestones; ensuring each is drafted, tested, and released on time
  • Coordinate testing/QA of assignments and autograders before release, working directly with TAs and the course instructor
  • Manage course infrastructure (Moodle, autograding platforms, Ed forums, GitHub Classroom, etc.)
  • Track and triage student questions/issues during the semester, escalating to TAs or instructor as needed
  • Support exam logistics: room booking, proctoring schedules, grading coordination
  • Cross-course and cross-faculty coordination
  • Serve as a liaison between the NLP and AIMM Labs's courses and other AI-related courses/faculty at EPFL, surfacing opportunities to share materials, infrastructure, or TA capacity
  • Contribute to planning for the new AI-101 bachelor's course ahead of its Fall 2027 launch, including TA team build-out and initial infrastructure setup

Requisiti principali

  • University degree (Bachelor's or Master's) in Computer Science, Data Science, or a related technical field
  • Prior experience as a TA, head TA, or in course/program coordination, ideally in a technical or CS-related field

Azienda e contesto

  • Strong organizational skills and comfort managing multiple parallel timelines and stakeholders
  • Working familiarity with tools such as Moodle, GitHub/GitHub Classroom, and autograding systems (or willingness to learn quickly)
  • Some technical fluency in NLP/ML concepts is a strong plus, though this is not a research or teaching role Fluent in English;
  • French is an asset for interacting with EPFL's administration
  • Reliable, proactive, and comfortable working directly with faculty, PhD students, and undergraduate TAs What We Offer
